# Layer 5: Financial Privacy

**Adversary:** Centralized data breaches,financial surveillance, and insecure fiat payment gateways.

In traditional finance, your transaction history is controlled by third parties such as banks, credit card processors,and in the worst case scenario data brokers who monetize that data for their own good. All of these act as a single point of failure. Furthermore, centralized cryptocurrency exchanges are frequent targets for hackers and are heavily regulated requiring extensive KYC data, completely defeating the point of a "decentralized medium of exchange".

This layer focuses on **Financial Security and Cryptographic Privacy**. It documents my use of decentralized peer to peer systems and privacy preserving cryptocurrencies to ensure my financial data remains secure and resilient against centralized breaches.
