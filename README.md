# My-Personal-OPSEC-Stack

Welcome to my documentation of the personal digital security and privacy architecture I use day to day to stay safe online and offline!

The Threat Model
----------------

Operational Security is meaningless without knowing the correct threat model in which you are defending against. My personal threat model is what I like to call a TIER 2 THREAT. This means that I am defending against ISPs, or internet service providers, opportunistic malicious actors, data brokers, and physical theft when I am out in public. This guide is NOT geared towards nation-state actors, hardware supply chain intersection, or TEMPEST attacks. If you need a guide on that you can go to the dark web or something adjacent lol. 

The Philosophy Behind the Scene
--------------------------------

My main goal behind what I am doing here is maximum attack surface reduction without hindering daily usability. Of course this does not mean everything will be the same after implementing these practices of course they won't because as the saying goes the more privacy adjacent you become the less convenient your experience becomes also but my exact practices are meant to make you more private while making the experience as usable as it can possibly be. I don't believe in adding friction just for the sake of it so every tool in this stack is only there to mitigate a specific, realistic threat.
