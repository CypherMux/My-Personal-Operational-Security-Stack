# Layer 4: Identity and Credentials

**Adversary:** Credential stuffing, phishing attacks, keyloggers, SIM swapping, and physical coercion.

In the modern digital landscape, your identity **IS** your credentials. If an attacker gets your master password or bypasses the 2FA on your account they don't just steal your data, they become you. They can drain your bank accounts, hijack your social media, or even completely destroy your reputation.

This layer is about **Identity Sovereignty and Zero Trust**. I do not and never will rely on SMS for 2FA, I do not use biometrics, and I will never store my passwords in the cloud.
